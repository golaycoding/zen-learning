# Quiz: 8 Parts of Speech

## Task

Create a 20-question multiple-choice grammar quiz for a 7th grader based on the 8 parts of speech taught in this YouTube video: [The 8 Parts of Speech in English Grammar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=chjmnCSPnbw).

If you are unable to get the transcript from the given YouTube video, use the `the-8-parts-of-speech-in-english-grammar.md` file.

## Content Coverage

Cover all 8 parts of speech with a mix of question types:

- Identifying the part of speech of a bolded/underlined word in a sentence
- Choosing the correct word to complete a sentence
- Fill-in-the-blank
- Multi-word spotting (identifying every instance of a given part of speech in a sentence)

Distribute questions across:

- **Nouns** — people, places, things/concepts
- **Pronouns** — personal, demonstrative, indefinite, interrogative, reflexive, relative
- **Adjectives** — including correct adjective order
- **Verbs** — dynamic, stative, auxiliary, modal, phrasal
- **Adverbs** — modifying verbs, adjectives, other adverbs, or a whole sentence
- **Prepositions**
- **Conjunctions** — coordinating/FANBOYS, correlative, subordinating
- **Articles** — definite ("the") vs. indefinite ("a"/"an")

## Formatting Requirements

- Print-friendly and ink-saving: no background colors, no tables, plain black text, Arial font, US Letter page size
- Each question should have four options labeled a) b) c) d)
- Distribute the correct answers evenly between a, b, c, d options (earlier all correct answers were concentrated at b)
- Header should include the title, source video credit, grade level, and a line for Name / Date / Score
- Include an answer key on a separate page showing the correct letter and a one-sentence rule-based explanation for every question
- Every page should have a title at the top and a page number at the bottom, formatted as current page of total pages (e.g. "1 of 10")
- Export as a `.docx` file
